About

Who you will work with

I'm Paulo Pedrosa Jr. I help companies and professionals organize their digital presence and grow without losing direction.

I combine corporate experience, analytical thinking, creativity and technology. I take the work seriously, but I believe good results come from curiosity, frank conversation and continuous learning. Every client has a different context, so I start by listening.

My background

Paulo Pedrosa Junior

I spent over 30 years in complex corporate environments, almost all in the automotive industry: business development, negotiation, commercial strategy and processes.

Moving into digital was the natural continuation of that path. I took with me the big-picture view, the discipline of execution and the habit of solving hard problems that I learned in industry.

Today I help companies and professionals connect strategy, brand, content, traffic and technology into a more coherent digital presence.

My work doesn't start with a tool. It starts with understanding the business, the market, the people and the result that needs to be built.

What guides my work

Respect — I treat people, teams and partners with consideration, even in difficult moments. Trust — clear agreements, transparent communication. Relationship — I prefer long partnerships to one-sale relationships. Humanization — I use technology to bring people closer and to clarify, not to create distance. Balance — I look for results without losing sight of the sustainability of decisions and relationships. Continuous learning — I question, test and evolve all the time.
How I work

Strategy that connects people, processes and technology

My digital traction approach improves the way a company presents itself, relates to its audiences and grows online.

Instead of treating ads, social media, brand, content and CRM as separate actions, I look at how they work together for the goals of the business.

I can work on:

  • Brand positioning and strategy
  • Structuring the digital presence
  • Campaign management and optimization
  • Content and creative direction
  • Social media and audience relationships
  • CRM, automation and digital processes
  • Business development and market expansion
  • Project management and team coordination

I decide based on data, context and experience. Indicators guide the way, but they don't replace judgment or conversation with the people involved.

When a project needs more people, I work with a network of trusted partners chosen for that job. You always talk to me.

"There are no heroes in entrepreneurship. There are committed people, relationships of trust and cohesive teams."

The best result comes from experience, collaboration and shared responsibility. I come in as a strategic and practical partner, respecting what the company already knows and helping turn problems into concrete next steps.
