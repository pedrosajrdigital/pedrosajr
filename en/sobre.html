Background

Paulo Pedrosa Junior

Paulo Pedrosa Junior spent over 30 years in complex corporate environments, almost all in the automotive industry: business development, negotiation, commercial strategy and processes.

Moving into digital was the natural continuation of that path, bringing along the big-picture view, the discipline of execution and the habit of solving hard problems learned in industry.

Today, strategy, brand, content, traffic and technology are connected into a more coherent digital presence for companies and professionals.

The work doesn't start with a tool. It starts with understanding the business, the market, the people and the result that needs to be built.

Values

Respect — people, teams and partners treated with consideration, even in difficult moments. Trust — clear agreements, transparent communication. Relationship — long partnerships are worth more than one-sale relationships. Humanization — technology to bring people closer and to clarify, not to create distance. Balance — results without losing sight of the sustainability of decisions and relationships. Continuous learning — questioning, testing and evolving all the time.
