Mercedes-Benz

Account recovery Commercial negotiation

Mercedes-Benz had cut its purchases from my company from 100% to 5% of demand, shifting its focus to other brands. I was given the task of rebuilding that relationship. I found that my company's product cost 50% more than the competition's and that the dealerships lacked technical support. I fixed both and resumed negotiations at several levels of the customer. Share went from 5% to 30% in the first months, and reached 50% after about six months of direct follow-up.

Ford — Fiesta / EcoSport

Technical failure Recall avoided

After the launch of the Fiesta and the EcoSport, a bearing on the secondary shaft began to fail. Ford wanted my company to bear the cost of a recall. I spent days on a test bench until I found the real cause: the aluminum housing was not rigid enough in one specific area, which deformed the bearing during assembly. Instead of redesigning the whole part — expensive and slow — I proposed a steel insert only at that point. The solution worked, avoided the recall, and my company was even chosen to manufacture the insert.
