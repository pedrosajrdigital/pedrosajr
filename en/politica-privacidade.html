Privacy Policy

Transparency, respect and security in how your data is handled.

Last updated: October 2026

The privacy and security of your data matter to me, Paulo Pedrosa Jr - Digital Traction Engineering. This Privacy Policy describes how I collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you interact with this site, in line with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD - Law 13.709/2018).

1. Personal Data Collection

I collect the information you give me directly when using this site, especially when filling out the contact form or requesting a proposal. The data collected may include:

  • Full name and/or company name;
  • Work email address;
  • Phone number or WhatsApp;
  • Professional information and details about your business challenges.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

The data collected is used exclusively for the following purposes, always with the legitimate interest of improving my services and establishing business contact:

  • Answering questions, requests and contacts made through this site;
  • Carrying out technical assessments and sending digital traction proposals;
  • Keeping you updated on project progress or service updates;
  • Improving the browsing experience on this site.

3. Use of Cookies and Tracking Technologies

To optimize site performance, I use statistics cookies (Google Analytics). These small text files help me understand how visitors interact with the pages, providing access metrics (such as most visited pages and time on site) anonymously. These cookies only load after you accept the notice shown on the site; you can decline, change your mind later, or set your browser to block them, without losing access to any part of the site.

4. Data Sharing

I guarantee that I never sell, rent or trade your personal data under any circumstances. Your information is kept confidential and is only shared with:

  • Trusted partners I may bring into a specific project, strictly for the technical analysis of that work;
  • Third-party management systems (CRM, email, contact form) I use to run my business, which also comply with privacy laws;
  • Legal authorities, in case of a substantiated court order requiring such disclosure.

5. Information Security

I adopt high-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, destruction or alteration. The site uses a secure connection (SSL) and the services I use have restricted logical access.

6. Your Rights (LGPD)

As the data subject, you have the right, at any time and upon request, to:

  • Confirm whether your data is being processed;
  • Access the information I hold about you;
  • Request correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • Request the anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data;
  • Withdraw consent previously given.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your data rights, reach out by email: tracione@pedrosajr.com.br.
